Biography-
Hello. We are Confoederatio. We are a mapping/tech collective that works on a wide range of software and dataset projects, with the endgoal of constructing an open-source digital twin of Earth from 10000BC to Today.

If this website looks rough, that's because it is constantly being iterated and improved, so it may lag several months behind our current projects due to time constraints. We always try to address these issues as soon as possible.

You can contact us at vf@confoederatio.org, as well as our either over our Discord or Confoederatio Element.
  • Documentation: https://docs.confoederatio.org/
  • GitHub: @Confoederatio
  • GitLab: Confoederatio
  • YouTube: @Confoederatio

Our current flagship projects can be found bookmarked above. We also manage long-run, multivariate datasets for borders, cities, economics, and population. Most of our tools can be found over Unitel.

Confoederatio's underpinnings mainly go back to grand-strategy modding communities, particularly amongst 11:59 and AOC2/AOC3 with Midnight from 2019-2020. As these projects no longer became viable, much of the community's work was forked to independent grand-strategy projects such as Triumph & Tragedy, and later to applied modelling and geospatial statistics.

Confoederatio was formed mainly as a single merger of a bunch of disparate projects within Midnight between similar members in 2023, and can be split into three or four main branches, namely:
  • CAD (Confoederatio, Aristic Division)
  • CRD (Confoederatio, Research Division)
  • CTD (Confoederatio, Technical Division)
  • CTD Games (Games Division)
This in addition to the Preservés des Confoederatio, which operates our physical/digital archives that we are currently working on migrating.
